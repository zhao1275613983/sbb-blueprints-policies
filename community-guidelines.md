# SBB Blueprints Creator & Community Guidelines

Effective date: September 9, 2026

SBB Blueprints exists to help builders create, share, discover, and recreate
great builds. It must not become a tool for taking credit, attention, or income
away from the people who created them.

Respect the original creator. If you use a blueprint, please visit the
creator's original community page, profile, or work page and leave some
positive support—a like, comment, follow, or other meaningful feedback. If a
creator offers paid work or services, follow their sharing, use, and payment
rules.

## 1. Share your own work or get permission

- Publish your own build, or obtain the original creator's clear permission
  before publishing their work as a blueprint.
- Manually rebuilding a design from a video, stream, screenshot, or tutorial
  does not make that design your own.
- Credit alone is not permission. If a creator has published a license that
  allows redistribution, follow every condition of that license.

## 2. Credit creators and send attention back to them

- Clearly name the original creator whenever a blueprint is based on their
  work.
- Link to the creator's original post, video, profile, community, or work page
  whenever possible.
- Do not present someone else's work as your own, remove their attribution, or
  use a blueprint to bypass access to paid or private work.
- Do not redistribute paid, commissioned, supporter-only, or otherwise
  restricted work unless the creator has explicitly allowed it.

## 3. Test every blueprint before publishing

- Test the exact blueprint code you are submitting—not an earlier copy.
- Confirm that the complete blueprint can be placed successfully in one
  attempt under the environment stated in the post, without being rejected
  partway through.
- Accurately list the game version, Vanilla or Modded status, required mods,
  relevant server settings, and any important placement requirements.
- If the blueprint later becomes broken or outdated, update its status, fix
  it, or remove it.

## 4. Handle problems through the publisher first

- If a blueprint does not work, contact its publisher in the Reddit post first.
  Include your game version, mod list, server environment, error message, and
  what happened during placement.
- The publisher should check the stated requirements and try to reproduce the
  problem.
- If the publisher confirms that the problem comes from SBB rather than the
  blueprint, they should collect the details and report them to the mod author.
- Suspected copying, unauthorized redistribution, malicious content, or
  harassment may be reported directly to the community moderators instead.

## 5. Keep the community constructive

- Discuss problems respectfully. Do not harass creators, publishers, users, or
  mod developers.
- Moderators may ask for proof of permission or testing, mark a blueprint as
  broken, remove a post from the community, or restrict repeat offenders.
- Good-faith mistakes should be corrected. Deliberate plagiarism,
  misrepresentation, or redistribution of restricted work may result in
  immediate removal.

## Reporting and contact

Use Reddit's native report tools or send Modmail to the community moderators.
Include the blueprint post and the original source when reporting unauthorized
redistribution.

If you are the original creator and find your work published in this community
without your permission, report the post or contact the community moderators
through Modmail. Please provide the blueprint post, your original work, and any
information that helps demonstrate authorship or usage rights. Moderators will
review the claim, may request additional information, and take appropriate
action on the reported post.

These guidelines supplement the [SBB Blueprints Terms of Use](terms.html) and
Reddit community rules. Data handling is described in the
[SBB Blueprints Privacy Notice](privacy.html).
